A closer look

One core, every place you work.

01

Profiles, applied in one click

A flat list of profiles — api-local, api-production, web-staging. Apply one and every new terminal gets it.

02

Undo puts it all back

Applying records what was there first, once. Undo restores those values exactly, including the ones that did not exist.

03

zsh, bash, fish and PowerShell

On macOS and Linux a guarded block in your startup file; on Windows your user environment in the registry. Nothing needs sudo.

04

The app, the command, two IDEs

The hyperenv command and the editor plugins drive the same core, store and journal as the apps — one writer, one state.
